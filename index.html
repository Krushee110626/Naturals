Our Story

At Krushee Naturals, we believe that the best things in life aren't manufactured—they are grown.

Based out of Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh, we grow and source plants, organic manure, and fresh fruits the way it has always made sense — naturally. We know where every product comes from, because most of it comes from our own hands.

Krushee Naturals was born out of a deep respect for nature and a mission to bring pure, naturally grown, and ethically sourced products directly to your doorstep.
